Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Household Admin helps you keep track of bills, renewals and subscriptions. To do that, we have to store some information about you. This policy explains what we store, why, and what we promise never to do with it.

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run your account and show you your own items.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers or anyone who wants to market to you.
  • We use no advertising, no analytics trackers and no third-party fonts or scripts in the app.
  • You can download or permanently delete all your data yourself, any time, from the Account screen.

Who we are

Household Admin is run by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"). For anything privacy-related, email [privacy@yourdomain.com].

What we collect, and why

  • Your name and email address. To create your account, let you log in, and contact you about your account if we have to.
  • Your password. We never store it in readable form. It is turned into a salted scrypt hash, so even we cannot see it.
  • The items you add. The name, type, amount, due date, repeat setting and optional note for each bill or renewal. This is the service itself: it is what lets you save your things and come back to them.
  • Account records. When you created your account and when you accepted this policy, so we can show we asked for your agreement.
  • A login cookie. One strictly necessary cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

What we do not collect

  • No bank login details, card numbers or account numbers.
  • No identity card or passport numbers.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No advertising profiles and no cross-site tracking.

How we use your information

Only to provide and secure the service: showing you your items, keeping your account safe, preventing abuse, and answering you if you contact us. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to the app.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for marketing. There are only two narrow exceptions:

  • Our hosting provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER NAME]) stores the app and its data on our behalf. It may only handle your data to run our service, and it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for a short period for security and reliability.
  • Legal requirements. If a court or authority legally requires us to disclose information, we will do so only as far as the law demands, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

If we ever want to share your information in any other way, we will ask for your clear consent first and update this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your account, items and login sessions are removed from our live system straight away. Any backups are overwritten within [30] days.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • The app is served over HTTPS, so data is encrypted while it travels.
  • Login cookies are HttpOnly, so scripts on a page cannot read them.
  • Each account can only see its own items.

No online service can promise perfect security. If we learn of a breach that puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), and as a matter of principle wherever you live, you can:

  • Access your data: use "Download my data" on the Account screen.
  • Correct it: edit your items in the app, or email us to correct your name or email.
  • Delete it: use "Delete account" on the Account screen.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or emailing us.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first at [privacy@yourdomain.com]. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

Children

Household Admin is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy
